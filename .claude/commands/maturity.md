---
description: Undiscounted rent still to pay per entity, by when it falls due. The maturity analysis for the accounts and the bank.
---

Run `npm run lease -- maturity` or `--as-at=<year end> --entity=<code>`.

Present the buckets (within 1 year, 1 to 2, 2 to 5, over 5), the total, the liability, and the interest still to come (the difference). This is the table AASB 16 para 58 and IFRS 7 ask for, and the one a bank asks for in a covenant review.
