---
description: Write up the year-end leases note for one entity from the records, for the accountant to review.
---

Run `npm run lease -- draft-disclosure --entity=<code>` (or `--year=<YYYY>`).

Open the draft, check the numbers against `/disclosure`, and tell the operator what the accountant still adds: the comparative year, judgements on lease terms and the rate, and anything material outside the leases records.
