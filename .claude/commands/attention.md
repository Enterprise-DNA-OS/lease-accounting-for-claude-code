---
description: Everything missed, due within 30 days, waiting for a second person or breaching a rule, in one list.
---

Run `npm run lease -- attention`.

Present it in this order, one line each:
1. **Off the books or wrong.** Leases with no measurement, measurements waiting for approval, CPI or market reviews in effect with no new rent. Say what each one does to the balance sheet (the liability is missing or understated).
2. **Deadlines.** Option last days and bank guarantee expiries, soonest first, with the owner. A missed option last day is a business decision now: say so plainly and suggest asking the landlord and a lawyer, never back-dating a notice.
3. **Landlord notices.** Victorian section 28 and NSW section 44 findings: what the tenant may still be entitled to.
4. **Housekeeping.** Exemptions that no longer qualify, make good with no estimate, leases past their term, months not closed.

End with the three things to do first, each naming a person.
