---
description: Lease liabilities (current and non-current) and right-of-use assets per lease and per entity, at any date.
---

Run `npm run lease -- balances` (today) or `--as-at=<date>` (a month end, a year end). `--entity=<code>` for one entity.

Present the TOTAL rows first, one per entity in its own currency, then the leases behind them. Current is the part of the liability that falls due in the next 12 months. Note any lease that is not on the list because its measurement waits for approval.
